#Contributing

HADIA-KB welcomes contributions that expand, verify, or correct documented knowledge about Handia/Hadia/Haria.

All new information must be supported by verifiable evidence. Each contribution should include a reliable source or persistent identifier such as a DOI, the exact geographic context, the name or terminology used in the original source, and a concise description of the evidence.

Corrections to existing records are also welcome when supported by appropriate evidence.

Please do not submit copyrighted full-text publications. Provide bibliographic details, source links, persistent identifiers, and appropriately paraphrased information instead.

Every contribution is reviewed for accuracy, source quality, provenance, geographic context, duplication, and consistency before inclusion in HADIA-KB. Verified additions and corrections are incorporated into subsequent versioned releases so that previously published datasets remain reproducible.

Our principle is simple: every record should be traceable, verifiable, and reusable.
