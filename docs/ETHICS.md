# Ethics and Traditional-Knowledge Safeguards

Public availability does not make traditional knowledge ownerless. HADIA-KB stores source-linked summaries and bibliographic metadata; it does not claim ownership of community knowledge or third-party publications. Future contributions involving non-public community knowledge should require appropriate consent, attribution, benefit-sharing review and institutional ethics procedures where applicable.

The portal does not encourage alcohol consumption and does not provide clinical or medical advice. Safety, nutritional and bioactivity findings remain bounded to the cited study context.
