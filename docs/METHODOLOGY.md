# Evidence Methodology

HADIA-KB is a secondary-data evidence infrastructure. Sources are discovered using beverage-name and starter-culture aliases, then retained with their exact name and geography. Claims are concise paraphrases rather than copied article text. Each claim carries a `source_id`.

## Evidence safeguards
1. Preserve source terminology.
2. Preserve geographic scope.
3. Keep primary studies, reviews, government/legal documents and public cultural sources distinguishable.
4. Do not count duplicated or derivative sources as independent confirmation.
5. Separate composition, in-vitro findings, ethnomedicinal belief and clinical evidence.
6. Preserve disagreement instead of averaging incompatible measurements.
7. Treat web absence as a documentation gap, not an absence of community knowledge.

This v1.0 release is deliberately framed as a **curated public-evidence snapshot**, not as a claim to contain all oral or unpublished knowledge.
